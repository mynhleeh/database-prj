\chapter{Company background and scenario}\label{company-background-and-scenario}

\section{Overview}\label{overview}

A34Build is a general contractor handling civil infrastructure, commercial buildings, and industrial facilities. The company runs multiple active projects simultaneously across distributed job sites, each governed by contractual deadlines, fixed budgets, and statutory safety requirements. Daily operations coordinate field engineers, site managers, equipment operators, heavy machinery, specialized subcontractors, and bulk construction materials.

Because operations currently rely on spreadsheets and paper logs, project teams experience scheduling delays, budget overruns, on-site inventory loss, and delayed cost tracking. The target relational database centralizes project schedules, workforce assignments, equipment utilization, material procurement, subcontracting, and site safety compliance.

\section{Main business operations}\label{main-business-operations}

Database coverage spans seven operational areas:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Projects: Client contracts, job sites, project phases, and scheduled work packages.
\item
  Workforce: Job roles, reporting hierarchy, certifications, dependents, and task assignments.
\item
  Equipment: Owned and rented machinery, site deployments, operating hours, and maintenance logs.
\item
  Materials: Suppliers, purchase orders, site deliveries, stock levels, and task consumption.
\item
  Subcontractors: Trade specialties, subcontract agreements, warranty holdbacks, and safety ratings.
\item
  Project costs: Budget variance tracking across labor, equipment, materials, and subcontracts.
\item
  Safety: Site safety audits, inspection scores, and workplace incident reports.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Entity sets}\label{entity-sets}

\subsection{Strong entity sets}\label{strong-entity-sets}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  DEPARTMENT: Company organizational units (\allowbreak{}Civil Engineering, Heavy Fleet, Procurement, Safety, Finance).
\item
  EMPLOYEE: Full-time and field staff (\allowbreak{}engineers, managers, operators, inspectors).
\item
  CLIENT: Public agencies and private developers commissioning projects.
\item
  PROJECT: Construction contracts with defined budgets and delivery schedules.
\item
  PROJECT\_\allowbreak{}SITE: Physical work locations belonging to a project.
\item
  TASK: Work packages within a project phase assigned to workers.
\item
  EQUIPMENT: Heavy machinery and construction vehicles.
\item
  SUPPLIER: Vendors supplying raw materials and parts.
\item
  MATERIAL: Standard catalog items (\allowbreak{}cement, rebar, gravel, pipes).
\item
  PURCHASE\_\allowbreak{}ORDER: Procurement orders issued to suppliers.
\item
  SUBCONTRACTOR: External contractors hired for specialized trades.
\item
  SAFETY\_\allowbreak{}INSPECTION: Periodic site safety audits.
\item
  INCIDENT\_\allowbreak{}REPORT: Logs of workplace accidents, equipment damage, or near-misses.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Weak entity sets}\label{weak-entity-sets}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  DEPENDENT: Family members of an employee recorded for benefits and emergency contact (\allowbreak{}identifying entity: \texttt{EMPLOYEE}; identifying relationship: \texttt{HAS\_\allowbreak{}DEPENDENT}; partial key: \texttt{DependentName}).
\item
  PROJECT\_\allowbreak{}PHASE: Sequential stages within a project such as Foundation, Structure, and Finishing (\allowbreak{}identifying entity: \texttt{PROJECT}; identifying relationship: \texttt{CONSISTS\_\allowbreak{}OF\_\allowbreak{}PHASES}; partial key: \texttt{PhaseNumber}).
\item
  PO\_\allowbreak{}ITEM: Individual line items on a purchase order (\allowbreak{}identifying entity: \texttt{PURCHASE\_\allowbreak{}ORDER}; identifying relationship: \texttt{CONTAINS\_\allowbreak{}ITEM}; partial key: \texttt{LineNumber}).
\item
  MAINTENANCE\_\allowbreak{}RECORD: Service and repair logs for a machine (\allowbreak{}identifying entity: \texttt{EQUIPMENT}; identifying relationship: \texttt{HAS\_\allowbreak{}MAINTENANCE}; partial key: \texttt{RecordNumber}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Date and time rules}\label{date-and-time-rules}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Chronological endpoints must not precede start dates across all scheduled records: \texttt{PROJECT} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{StartDate \textless= PlannedEndDate}, \texttt{StartDate \textless= ActualEndDate}), \texttt{PROJECT\_\allowbreak{}PHASE} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{StartDate \textless= TargetCompletionDate}), \texttt{TASK} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{ScheduledStartDate \textless= ScheduledEndDate}, \texttt{ScheduledStartDate \textless= ActualEndDate}), \texttt{PURCHASE\_\allowbreak{}ORDER} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{OrderDate \textless= ExpectedDeliveryDate}), \texttt{EQUIPMENT\_\allowbreak{}DEPLOYMENT} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{DeploymentStartDate \textless= DeploymentEndDate}), \texttt{ENGAGES\_\allowbreak{}SUBCONTRACTOR} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{StartDate \textless= CompletionDate}), and \texttt{SAFETY\_\allowbreak{}INSPECTION} (\allowbreak{}\texttt{InspectionDate \textless= FixDeadline} when \texttt{FixDeadline} is non-null).
\item
  Workers must be at least 18 years old at hiring and at runtime (\allowbreak{}\texttt{CurrentDate - DOB \textgreater= 18 years} and \texttt{HireDate \textgreater= DOB + 18 years}).
\item
  Task \texttt{ScheduledStartDate} and \texttt{ScheduledEndDate} values must fall within the parent \texttt{PROJECT\_\allowbreak{}PHASE} interval (\allowbreak{}\texttt{StartDate} to \texttt{TargetCompletionDate}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Rules not modeled in ERD}\label{rules-not-modeled-in-erd}

These cross-entity and dynamic constraints cannot be expressed directly in Chen EER notation and require triggers, check constraints, or application logic:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Phase budget limit: The sum of \texttt{PhaseBudget} across all phases of a project cannot exceed \texttt{PROJECT.\allowbreak{}EstimatedBudget}.
\item
  No overlapping machine bookings: An \texttt{EQUIPMENT} unit cannot have overlapping \texttt{{[}DeploymentStartDate, DeploymentEndDate{]}} intervals across different sites in \texttt{EQUIPMENT\_\allowbreak{}DEPLOYMENT}.
\item
  Operator licensing: An employee linked via \texttt{OPERATES} to \texttt{EQUIPMENT\_\allowbreak{}DEPLOYMENT} must hold a matching equipment license in \texttt{EMPLOYEE.\allowbreak{}Certifications}.
\item
  Daily work limit: Total \texttt{HoursLogged} across all \texttt{WORKS\_\allowbreak{}ON\_\allowbreak{}TASK} records for a single employee on the same \texttt{AssignedDate} cannot exceed 12 hours.
\item
  No circular task dependencies: The recursive \texttt{PRECEDES} graph over \texttt{TASK} must remain a directed acyclic graph (\allowbreak{}DAG) with no self-loops or cycles.
\item
  Stock limit on consumption: \texttt{MATERIAL\_\allowbreak{}USAGE.\allowbreak{}QuantityUsed} for a given \texttt{(\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}MaterialID, SiteID)} cannot exceed \texttt{SITE\_\allowbreak{}INVENTORY.\allowbreak{}CurrentStockQuantity} at that site.
\item
  Single active project limit for directors: Although \texttt{DIRECTS\_\allowbreak{}PROJECT} is modeled as 1:1 in the conceptual schema, historical project records require enforcing that an employee directs at most one \texttt{In-Progress} project at any given time.
\item
  Accident emergency notification: When \texttt{INCIDENT\_\allowbreak{}REPORT} references an injured employee via \texttt{INVOLVES\_\allowbreak{}EMPLOYEE}, the system must query \texttt{DEPENDENT} where \texttt{IsEmergencyContact = true} to notify the designated emergency contact.
\end{enumerate}

\chapter{Entity-Relationship Diagram}\label{entity-relationship-diagram}

\section{Diagram overview and notation}\label{diagram-overview-and-notation}

In Chen EER notation, rectangles denote strong entity sets, double rectangles denote weak entity sets, diamonds denote regular relationships, and double diamonds denote identifying relationships. Ellipses represent attributes: underlined for primary keys, dashed-underlined for partial keys, double-lined for multi-valued attributes, and dashed-outlined for derived attributes. Double lines indicate total participation, while single lines indicate partial participation. Cardinalities are labeled \texttt{1}, \texttt{N}, \texttt{M}, and \texttt{P}. Dashed aggregation boxes enclose \texttt{EQUIPMENT\_\allowbreak{}DEPLOYMENT} and \texttt{DELIVERY\_\allowbreak{}DISPATCH} so those relationships participate directly in \texttt{OPERATES}, \texttt{FULFILLS\_\allowbreak{}PO}, and \texttt{RECEIVES\_\allowbreak{}DELIVERY}.

\section{Mapping rules and diagram}\label{mapping-rules-and-diagram}

Converting the Chen ERD yields 29 relation schemas under standard relational mapping rules: composite attributes (\allowbreak{}\texttt{Name}, \texttt{Address}, \texttt{LocationCoordinates}) expand into atomic scalar columns, multi-valued attributes (\allowbreak{}\texttt{ContactPhone}, \texttt{PhoneNumber}, \texttt{Certifications}, \texttt{SupplyCategories}) map to dedicated child tables keyed by owner key plus attribute value, and derived attributes (\allowbreak{}\texttt{Age}, \texttt{ProjectDuration}, \texttt{ActualCost}, \texttt{ActualHours}, \texttt{TotalRunHours}, \texttt{ItemSubtotal}, \texttt{TotalPOAmount}) are excluded from stored columns. Weak entities inherit their identifying owner's primary key combined with their partial key, while 1:1, 1:N, M:N, ternary, and aggregation relationships map via foreign keys or associative tables.
